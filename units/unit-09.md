The unit covered business continuity and disaster recovery planning, business impact analysis, and recovery time and recovery point objectives, with availability modelling of disaster-recovery-as-a-service (Andrade *et al.*, 2017).

### What the unit set out to teach

On completing the unit I should be able to:

- Explain what is meant by BC, DR, RTO and RPO.
- Suggest the most suitable solutions to meet specific recovery objectives.
- Discuss some of the factors driving the future of the Information Risk Management field.

## Artefact: Collaborative Discussion 2 summary post

My summary accepted Kaona's challenge that SSVC is not inherently more consistent than CVSS, because decision trees are only as reliable as their inputs. I kept SSVC as the better decision mechanism, citing CISA's operational adoption (CISA, 2022), but argued that it needs governance to avoid reproducing CVSS's subjectivity: an asset inventory linked to business services, documented rationale, second review for critical systems, reassessment as threat intelligence changes, and exploit-likelihood data such as EPSS (Jacobs *et al.*, 2021).
