The unit introduced quantitative risk modelling, including Monte Carlo simulation (Metropolis, 1987), Bayesian methods, and multi-criteria decision methods such as AHP and ANP, together with their limitations (Asadabadi, Chang and Saberi, 2019). Collaborative Discussion 2 began, on Wunder *et al.*'s (2024) study of CVSS scoring inconsistencies.

### What the unit set out to teach

On completing the unit I should be able to:

- Utilise quantitative risk modelling techniques to produce risk models.
- Select the most appropriate technique based on the real-world problem.
- Critically evaluate the pros and cons of the approach selected.

## Artefact: Collaborative Discussion 2 initial post

I argued that Wunder *et al.* (2024) rightly criticise CVSS for subjectivity, complexity and inconsistency between assessors, that technical severity is not the same as organisational priority, and that SSVC (Spring *et al.*, 2019) should replace CVSS as the primary basis for prioritisation, with CVSS retained as an input.
